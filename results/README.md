# Analysis Results

This folder contains summary tables and statistical outputs produced during the analysis of dairy calf feed intake and growth.

## Planned Results

The analysis notebook examines:

- Weekly body-weight patterns in Control and Hay calves.
- Observed weight change and endpoint-based average daily gain (ADG).
- Daily grain, hay, and total mixed ration (TMR) intake patterns.
- Exploratory relationships between feed intake, calf body weight, and age.
- Data quality, missing observations, and limitations of the available measurements.

## Interpretation Notes

- Results are descriptive unless a statistical test is explicitly reported.
- Feed intake and body weight measurements repeated over time for the same calf are not independent observations.
- Exploratory correlations do not establish causation.
- Missing intake values are retained as missing rather than automatically imputed.
- The endpoint-based ADG calculated in the notebook is distinct from the study-provided ADG measure.
